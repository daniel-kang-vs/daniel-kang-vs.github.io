Data Analyst

Required Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in analytics, business, or related field
- 1+ years experience in data analysis or business intelligence
- Strong SQL skills for querying and transforming data
- Python for data analysis and automation
- Power BI or Tableau for data visualization and dashboards
- Excel for analysis and reporting
- Experience building ETL data pipelines
- Statistical analysis and modeling
- Ability to communicate insights to technical and non-technical stakeholders

Preferred Qualifications:
- Microsoft Azure or AWS cloud experience
- A/B testing and experimentation experience
- Forecasting and predictive analytics
- Experience in manufacturing, healthcare, or retail
- Google Data Analytics or similar certification

Responsibilities:
- Build dashboards and KPI monitoring reports for business stakeholders
- Develop SQL-based analytics pipelines from behavioral and operational data
- Perform cohort, funnel, and segmentation analysis
- Support data-driven decision-making across acquisition, engagement, and retention
- Automate reporting and streamline insight generation
