Growth Analytics · Marketing · Experimentation

Jambo Club — Reducing Acquisition Cost and Recovering Retention

Business Context & Stakes

Leadership faced rising paid acquisition costs and weak early retention. Without a clear view of where users dropped off—and which channels actually drove incremental conversions— budget decisions risked wasting spend while churn quietly eroded lifetime value.

Data Pipeline

Extracted and structured 100K+ behavioral events with SQL and Python, cleaned event sequences for funnel stages, and built reusable ETL pipelines on AWS-backed workflows so marketing KPIs refreshed without manual pulls.

Methodology

Combined funnel and cohort analysis to isolate churn drivers, then designed geo-split experiments and multivariate bid tests with regression-based lift to validate incrementality—not just correlational “wins.” Defined acceptance criteria and validated outputs before recommendations reached leadership.

Business Insights & Quantified Impact

  • Raised 30-day retention from 22% to 53% through targeted retention initiatives.
  • Improved customer acquisition efficiency by 27% CPC reduction while holding conversion volume.
  • Cut recurring reporting cycle time by 60% via automated KPI dashboards for stakeholders.
Stack: SQL · Python · AWS · Funnel / Cohort · A/B Testing · Dashboards Experience timeline

Business Insights & Quantified Impact

  • Reduced manual reporting time by 40% with automated B2B KPI dashboards.
  • Enabled budget reallocation toward highest-ROI B2B segments using quantified tradeoff scenarios.

Business Context & Stakes

Data Pipeline

Methodology

Applied newsvendor-style optimization and demand forecasting to recommend pre-shift positioning, then stress-tested scenarios so stakeholders could see revenue upside versus imbalance risk.

Business Insights & Quantified Impact

  • Projected ~$12.4M / year revenue gain from improved zone positioning.
  • Translated a dense logistics dataset into an actionable fleet allocation narrative for operators.
  • Demonstrated how prescriptive analytics turns demand forecasts into capacity decisions.
Stack: Python · Optimization · Demand Forecasting · Scenario Analysis Live project · GitHub

Predictive Analytics · Ensemble Modeling · Decision Systems

NCAA Selection Model — Building a High-Accuracy Decision Support Score

Business Context & Stakes

Tournament selection is a high-stakes ranking problem under incomplete information— analogous to business decisions where committees must prioritize candidates with noisy, multi-signal inputs. Assembled multi-season team performance features, engineered selection-relevant signals, and built a reproducible Python ensemble pipeline with holdout evaluation.

Methodology

Used a stacked ensemble to predict selection probability and seed placement, emphasizing calibration near the cutline where ranking errors are most costly.

Business Insights & Quantified Impact

  • Reached AUC 0.966 for selection prediction—strong discrimination for prioritization use cases.
  • Predicted seed placement within two lines, supporting “shortlist confidence” narratives similar to business tiering.
  • Reframes sports ML as a transferable decision-support pattern: multi-signal ranking under uncertainty.
Stack: Python · Ensemble ML · Ranking / Classification Live project · GitHub
