Analysis

Methodology, interpretation and limitations

Method

From data to decision

  1. Aggregate historical trips into zone and time-window demand.
  2. Translate missed-trip and idle-vehicle costs into allocation decisions.
  3. Compare empirical, predict-then-optimize and decision-focused approaches on the same 35 scenarios.
  4. Inspect fixed-fleet and adjustable-fleet scenarios using total cost, including rental adjustments.

Interpretation

What to take away

In the committed 35-scenario bakeoff, the empirical method has the lowest mean newsvendor cost: about $369,468 per scenario. Its mean fill rate is 55.4%. These are modeled evaluation outputs, not realized savings. The dashboard shows the exact committed comparison and three stakeholder slots.

Limitations & next steps

The outputs are historical scenarios with modeled cost assumptions. Repositioning time, demand shifts and operational constraints require further validation. A local agent prototype exists in the source; this does not establish deployment or live dispatch use.
