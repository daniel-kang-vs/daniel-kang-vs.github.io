Fleet Optimization

NYC TLC has a fixed fleet of 13,000 yellow cabs. The question isn’t whether to deploy — it’s where to pre-position them before each shift, when every missed fare and every idle cab carry different costs.

The Problem

Two challenges before we can optimize

Trip data only records completed yellow pickups — not people who couldn’t find a cab. We reconstruct true demand using for-hire vehicle (Uber/Lyft) data, then solve a newsvendor allocation that penalizes missed high-fare trips more than idle cabs.

Observed vs. Estimated Demand by Borough

Gray = yellow pickups only. Gold = estimated total demand after FHV proxy. Outer boroughs are especially undercounted.

Asymmetric Cost per Trip

A missed Midtown fare (~$17) hurts more than an idle cab (~$12). This drives cabs toward airports and high-fare zones.

Demand Model Bake-off

Which forecast feeds the optimizer best?

We tested four demand models across 35 time-slot scenarios. Lower newsvendor (NV) cost means better fleet placement. The simple empirical quantile model won — ML alternatives overfit thin per-zone samples.
